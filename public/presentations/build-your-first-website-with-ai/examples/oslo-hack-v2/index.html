A small idea is enough.

Pick an idea, build a first version, and test it with someone. Keep what works. Change one thing and try again.

Bring a laptop, a charger, and your curiosity. No coding experience is required.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need coding experience?

No. Start with one small idea and learn as you build.

What should I bring?

A laptop, a charger, and something you are curious about.

Can I come without an idea?

Yes. Join someone else's project or try the workshop example.
